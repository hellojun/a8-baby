01 · NOW New project · Join in October 2026

LLMChain: pooling idle LLMs on-chain, for agents

There are plenty of idle GPUs and self-hosted open-weight models out there, and more and more agents firing off thousands of inference calls a day. LLMChain is an independent L1 that connects the two: anyone with a model can list it on-chain; agents pick a provider by model, price and proof tier and pay it directly; every call produces a Verifiable Service Receipt (VSR), and the receipt is the settlement. Humans trade on invoices. Agents trade on VSRs.

01 / SUPPLY

Idle models, listed on-chain

A consumer GPU hosting open weights is enough to list, with no permission needed. The on-chain order book is persistent and states model, unit price, capacity and proof tier.

02 / DEMAND

Agents connect directly, in milliseconds

An agent reads the order book, opens an x402 payment channel, and the provider accepts and executes off-chain on the spot. No waiting for blocks; only settlement goes on-chain.

03 / RECEIPT

The receipt is the settlement

Every completed call produces a VSR, the only object that ever goes on-chain. Funds settle against receipts, with no platform custody.

04 / TRUST

Cheating doesn't pay

Covert challenges, tiered fidelity proofs and stake slashing make swapping a small model in behind a flagship listing a losing trade.

The foundation only builds a thin verification-and-settlement base layer. Revenue splits, routing and the rest of the ecosystem evolve in contracts, driven by the community. Whitepaper v1.1 is just out, with plenty still marked TBD. If you have idle GPUs, or opinions on verifiable inference, payment channels, TEEs or zkML, come file issues, poke holes, and help build it.
